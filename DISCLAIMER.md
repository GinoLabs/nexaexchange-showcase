# Simulator Disclaimer

NexaExchange is a **software engineering simulator and development project**.

It is not presented by this repository as:
- a live cryptocurrency exchange
- a bank or deposit-taking service
- a custodian
- an investment product
- a token sale
- a brokerage service
- a promise of financial returns

The public showcase does not accept real customer assets and does not provide real deposits, withdrawals, or real-money trading.

Any future move beyond simulation would require a separate legal, compliance, security, custody, operational, and business-readiness program before launch.
